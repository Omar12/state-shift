## Design Context

**Brand:** Warm · Grounded · Quiet — like a trusted friend who doesn't overreact. No hype, no affirmations.

**Audience:** General adults catching a bad moment anywhere — pragmatists, not wellness enthusiasts.

**Aesthetic:** Warm editorial. Off-white/cream background, earthy warm neutrals, typography-led, generous spacing. Feels like a quality print piece. Light mode only. WCAG AA required. Custom fonts OK.

**Anti-references:** No pastel wellness gradients, no dark mode glows, no glassmorphism, no AI color palette (cyan/purple).

**Principles:** Calm through confidence · Typography is the UI · One thing at a time · Warmth from texture not color · No decorative noise
