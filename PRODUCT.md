# State Shift — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
General adults catching a bad moment at any point in the day — at work, at home, on transit. Not a specific wellness demographic. The app must work for someone who wouldn't normally open a wellness product: skeptics, pragmatists, someone just having a rough afternoon. Context of use is often brief, mobile, slightly stressed.

### Brand Personality
**Warm · Grounded · Quiet**

Like a trusted friend who doesn't overreact. No hype, no affirmations, no clinical distance. The voice is plain-spoken and steady. It doesn't try to make you feel better with words — it gives you something to do. Reassuring through competence, not through softness.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Warm editorial** — cream/off-white tones, organic warmth, feels like a thoughtfully designed print piece. Not pastel-wellness, not techy-minimal. Think: a well-designed independent magazine or a quality paperback. Readable, intentional, unhurried.

- Typography-led rather than color-led
- Off-white/cream background (never pure white)
- Earthy, warm neutrals — no cool grays
- Generous spacing that communicates calm
- Custom fonts welcomed — should feel considered, not default
- Light mode only
- WCAG AA compliance required

### Anti-references
- No pastel gradient wellness apps (Calm, Headspace aesthetic)
- No dark mode with glowing accents
- No rounded bubbly playfulness
- No clinical / medical UI
- No AI slop: no cyan accents, no purple-to-blue gradients, no glassmorphism

### Design Principles
1. **Calm through confidence** — the design doesn't try hard; it just works
2. **Typography is the UI** — hierarchy and type weight carry most of the communication load
3. **One thing at a time** — never more than one primary action visible
4. **Warmth from texture, not color** — off-whites, warm neutrals, never cool
5. **No decorative noise** — every element earns its place or gets removed
